Privacy Policy for ProfPlanner

Last updated: October 6, 2026

ProfPlanner ("we", "our", or "the app"), developed by Said Bara, is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding data privacy and security when using the ProfPlanner mobile application.

1. Offline-First & Local Storage

ProfPlanner is designed with an offline-first architecture:

  • Local Data Storage: All data entered into the application—including teacher profiles, student contacts, schools, timetables, lesson records, attendance logs, and financial transactions—is stored strictly locally on your device.
  • No Server Collection: We do not operate remote databases that collect, transmit, store, or sell your personal information or your students' data.
  • No Third-Party Analytics or Ads: ProfPlanner contains zero advertisements, tracking cookies, or commercial telemetry SDKs.

2. Permissions

ProfPlanner may request the following permissions:

  • Notifications: To deliver local alerts and reminders for your upcoming scheduled classes and sessions. Notification data remains entirely on your device.

3. Data Ownership & Deletion

You have full ownership of your data at all times:

  • You can create, modify, or permanently delete any session, student, school, or transaction within the app.
  • Clearing application storage or uninstalling ProfPlanner will permanently erase all data stored by the app on your device.

4. Children’s Privacy

ProfPlanner is an administrative tool intended for educators, teachers, and tutors aged 18 and older. It does not solicit or collect personal data from minors.
